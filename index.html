The mind behind it

About Me

Hi, I'm Peace — a 26-year-old web developer.

I didn't come into tech through a conventional route, and I'm not interested in pretending otherwise. I've built Karuva Tech while holding down a day job, freelancing on the side, and learning often the hard way — what it actually takes to deliver good work for clients who are trusting me with their business's first impression. Today, I work as a Web Developer and Digital Solutions Consultant — building not just websites, but the systems and workflows behind them.

I started Karuva Tech because I kept meeting business owners in Port Harcourt who wanted a website but didn't know who to trust, didn't understand what they were paying for, and had been quoted prices with zero explanation of what was actually included. I wanted to offer something different: the same patience, clarity, and care whether you're a one-person business or a growing company.

I'm also a Nigerian woman building a tech practice in a space that doesn't always expect to see me here. I don't say that for sympathy — I say it because it shapes how I work. I know what it feels like to walk into a room and have to prove you belong there. So when you work with me, you won't get spoken down to, rushed through a technical explanation you didn't ask for, or made to feel like your questions are a bother. You'll get someone who explains exactly what she's doing and why, and who takes your business as seriously as you do.
